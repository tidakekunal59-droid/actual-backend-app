Why is it chaotic?

The double pendulum is governed by strict deterministic equations (Lagrangian mechanics). However, it is highly sensitive to initial conditions. A tiny difference between the main and ghost pendulums (like 0.001 radians) will eventually cause their paths to diverge entirely. This is the hallmark of chaos.
